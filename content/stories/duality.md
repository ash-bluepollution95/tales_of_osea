Something was amiss.  The doors, the gates had become stale, almost out of order in their existence. No single voice was distinct this time, and things were starting to spiral. Vertigo was setting in. Not of disorder, not of chaos born from self inflicted means, but of hysteria and a sense of panic.  Sharing the supper, and becoming deathly ill from a simple prepared dish was not on tonight’s cards. Another night such as this, had happened in the past - but was of their own doing.


An Ancient that was known for their physical duality, as well as their mental - was slowly watching the night turn into their last. Someone was out to prepare war, an outright war to usher in a new era that was to serve a god that should never have come forth. A hallucination, a split in consciousness was normally controllable, but the seconds passed and the mind had become nothing less than the shattered glass of a broken mirror.


Standing, leaning almost against a pillar, in what seemed to be one of many of the gate rooms that he once kept watch. Heavy breathing, coughing up what seemed to likely be material of the color of blood. Footsteps approached, making the confusion of the moment steeped in anxiety, putting the suffering Ancient on guard.  Unable to see much past the strands of the hair in his face, he tried to look towards the sound of an oncoming fellow.


“Ianitos…” A familiar tone spoke out through the dusty mist.


“I cannot see. Let yourself be known, speak.”  A voice, of more than one tone, replied in kind. The plural was evident, the control was gone.  The ancient himself, the one with the shaggy rouge, charcoal colored mane. A softer face, one that molded with the change of pace, and transitioned through the seasons. A voice that was distinct yet ever diverse in every day in the calendar.


“There’s no time… The final days.. They’re starting.” A reply was sent, but no name was given. 


“Speak your name, I cannot see.” A growling sound exited the darkened mane’s mouth, partially of sheer confusion and partially of sheer frustration. There wasn’t anything making sense here. 


“It is I, Hythlodaeus.” A lavender braided, soft spoken archer, one that the black haired one was all too familiar with. Not for romantic reasons, but for friendship, for a kinship that left him confused as to why the messenger was this man.


“Ianitos… I am. But Ianitos I am not…” 


“You’re confused, you’ve been poisoned.” Hythlodaeous was sitting right in front of Ianitos at this moment, creating a strained moment, a pause - a sense of distance, for there was nothing more confusing in the moment than hearing your own friend speak of your ongoing demise.


“I.. have seen this. Before… When … I did this to myself, I - I lost control and communicated outside of our world.  We have time - do we not?”  Ianitos was clearly nearing the end of his own stages of life, let alone the doom to befall the world before them. It was unclear if the situation he was speaking of was that of another world, or just his mind’s eye.


“Ianitos. You’ve lost control, this time of ill reasons, malice has come for your duality and plurality - I am unsure where or who would do this to you.” Hythlodaeus offered a small potion over towards Ianitos and smiled. “This may at least aid your suffering and buy you some time hopefully.” 
“Suffering.” Ianitos blinked for a moment, “There’s suffering?”


“You, you’re suffering are you not?” Hythlodaeus tilted his head and grasped at a small handkerchief in his pocket. Moving it towards the mouth of his fellow Ancient, he wiped away blood from someone he once felt was like a brother.


“The fate of all of the future, the universes and worlds I have seen, there is much change ahead.. I cannot see past the strands of mine eyes, yet you would not tell me what is really going on? Do you take me for a fool?”  A switch, in Ianitos’ mind, in his voice lead the situation to become tangled, not only in confusion but in wild goose chasing. 


Hythlodaeus was one of few that catalogued the natural shifts of Ianitos, not because it was his job - but because it was like meeting a new friend every season.  Sometimes every morning, over coffee they would meet yet again.


“You have picked up on the essence of the worlds you’ve seen, worse off the ones from the pocket dimension you experimented with.” Hythlodaeus still was concerned, this was unlike Ianitos, uncontrollable and demanding.


Ianitos’s face shifted, his hair moving and travelling as the changes set in like the leaves on the Elpis Maple changing as Autumn rang forth. Yet stranger still, was the loud scream, and cry as if something was not only wrong, but pain was setting in faster and the time left was shorter than expected.  Scarier still, words that were coming from the Ancient’s mouth were of an unknown language, an unknown accent..


“FUCKING. CRISS YOU DID IT AGAIN.” 


And within seconds, the Ancient’s face returned back, his hair shifted back and his breathing laboured further.  Skin that was once healthy, started to shrink around his bones, pale and jaundice almost.  Ianitos was not a mage of the dark arts, he was secondary to gatekeeping a healer and a family leader.  He studied at times to be a Chirurgeon if the timing allowed him, alas he wasn’t in enough control most of the time. 


“Ianitos!” Hythlodaeus was set aback by the change in tone, the face difference and the evidence of an animalistic trait. He knew of experiments, he knew of the Entelechies, but not of a man with ears set in like a cat-like being. If this were before the end times, if this were only weeks prior, he would connect with Hermes - he would connect with anyone else that would be interested in this information..


“Yes?” Ianitos smiled, still unable to move much, and vision worsened, his complexion becoming paler the longer he stood against the pillar. “Ah, yes Hythlodaeus, my brother, are you well?” 


“You shifted. You spoke another language. You are not in control, you … you are dying and the rest of our world is dying with you… How can this be?” Hythlodaeus was confused as to why it was going to be Ianitos being the first to go. Why the gates would be left without their guard, and the tides of war and pestilence were to engulf without him. 


“As I normally would, it is in my nature I change with seasons – My winter form then my spring form to move…” Ianitos quickly was cut off before he started coughing yet again.


“Hush, please drink this.” Hythlodaeus presented the drink once more. 


“Alas.. Hythlodaeus we’re too far gone to be able to intake any solution to our ending, the poison wasn’t just from our meal. We’ve been ill for quite some time, almost as if a paradox within is creating a mutation that is blackening our organs and ashening us from the inside outwards.” Ianitos smiled, and sighed and slowly slid down the pole in the room he was leaning on and landed on the floor in a slump.  Finally sitting down in a somewhat more comfortable position he began to cough again.

“Ianitos, you were supposed to see someone about this the last time you spoke of it, what became of that?” Another figure stood in the doorway, one that spoke with more imminence in their voice than Hythlodaeus had previously. “We are running out of time, there are things we must finish before everything is engulfed.”


“Zodiark. I warned you, and I warned Venat about her plight – such Paradoxical and meaningless plights that serve nothing. I was to keep you all safe, warn you of war, bring to you peace.  Even Azem themselves was aware of the nightmares to come, and yet here you are rushing me in my own final days as if you were the one who darkened my ability to exist.” 


“Ianitos, that is enough.” The white haired figure sighed and walked forward and held a finger beneath the Ancient’s chin and frowned. “I was not the one who sired your illness, you had a hand in that all of yourself. At least of which the part that is slowly killing you, rather than the one that someone gave to you in your meal in hopes you’d die quicker.”


“Hades.” Ianitos frowned back, “I cannot even see past the strands of my lashes by now, and you expect me to remember my last visit with the healers? The last time I tried to cast cure on myself, I ended up with a nose pouring blood like a waterfall.”


“We have little time.”


“I am not your sacrificial lamb.”


“No, but you… likely won’t be surviving to become one, and I would rather bury you in the way you always wanted.  On your HEAD with your FEET six feet sticking up in the air just like Azem dared you to.” Hades shook his head and held his hand towards Ianitos and tried to aid him in standing the man upwards.


“I cannot walk. I cannot even stand anymore. I barely can even recall who I am, I started speaking in other tongues earlier.” Ianitos smiled, slipping his hand from Hades’ due to the lack of strength in his limbs.


“You have been doing that far longer than Hythlodaeus is aware, I caught your form shifting months ago and your tongue mirroring a language we’d never spoken.  You’ve seen your future, in every universe that you’re connected to, your experiment is what is killing you - akin to mold spores the paradox you’ve created has infected you.  You haven’t been at your post in over six months, the healers stopped being able to prevent the spread for over a month now.” Hades shook his head in disappointment, and knelt carefully in front of Ianitos and frowned yet once more.


“My friend. I regret, yet I do not regret seeing worlds flourish without our meddling- we have created chaos far beyond the limits we originally set our..” 


Ianitos began to cough again as his form and tongue shifted once more.


Hades raised his eyebrow and took a small chance at speaking with the shifted form, hoping to even in the end of days - gain insight. 


“Introduce yourself.”  Hades wasn’t able to match the lingua franca of the shifted form, but his body language was enough to stir. 


“Where am I…”  Ianitos’ body was strikingly different, shorter in form and stature, hair blue with pink ombre, glassy red eyes.  “Who.. who are you.”


“Introduce yourself.”  Hades repeated himself.


“I … I don’t understand what you’re asking me – where is here? – I don’t understand what you’re saying - it’s like you’re the fucking peanuts teacher going WA WA WA.” 


Hades ushered his hand as if to wave an introduction gesture, hoping to get across to this new form what he was asking. 


“Criss.” Ianitos blinked, “Criss X’voor. I just got out of math class - I gotta go pick Aeron up because we’re gonna go visit Kay across the portal for thanksgiving.” 


A smirk was across the white haired man’s face, painted thick and intrigued over the new findings. Sadly, the findings were likely going to be lost to time due to the oncoming and ongoing path of destruction.
