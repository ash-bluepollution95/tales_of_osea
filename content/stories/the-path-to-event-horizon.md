Have you ever stood in front of a window and just stared outside? The way that, in short, makes you envy the world and society around you. At the same time you’re watching a large tsunami come through, and you’re powerless as it engulfs everything around you. The water somehow doesn’t swallow you whole. At least not at first, not before everything goes dark and then you’ve realized you’re already gone.


I was at that window; the panes felt like they had maintained through the pressure of the oncoming water. The pressure wasn’t just from water coming through, it was the pressure of an existing event, a world swallowing paradoxical storm. One of my parents started this paradox by accident all the way back when I was a child. The first path towards the end of my future, my present and erasing my past.


I’ve been repeating this scene over and over again for over three years in almost real time at this stage. I should in theory be entirely erased, no longer in existence, as the universe that swallowed mine whole has my half-mirror, and my half-brother.  Until it hit, and stopped swallowing me and I saw something else. 


“Child, you are of our kind, something has come from your worlds and has started to cause changes.” 


A vision, a person almost - no portal, no nothing. I was hearing someone for the first time in years. Another of our kind, an Osenayan has found me somehow.  Why, I'm not sure and I'm unsure where they’re from. They likely are some form of an ancestor, but I don’t recall having telephone access to anyone past my grandparent’s age.


“Child, can you hear me?”


“I can sorta, but I swear to fuck I’m dead. My universe folded in on itself, you’re an imagination and my mom never came home.”


Laughing started erupting from the soundwaves that broke through the darkness now surrounding me. This laughing, was a heavy hearty force coming from the vision. I don’t know though, have you ever seen a glitching hologram in your mind start laughing at something you said?


“Child, you have been dead. We need you, and you need us. Another paradox is forming, and it is of malice.” 


I felt like punching something, but it’s not like I can even feel where my limbs are at this stage. I honestly at this stage could be a rotting corpse, and I'd never realize it. The all consuming power of trying to save your family members, the power and strength of fighting the cosmos just to keep your lineage from dying out. 


I don’t get why another paradox COULD happen, my universe went kaput, fizzled out like a bad sparkler on the fourth of July. You couldn’t even explain this to someone like Neil Degrasse Tyson, you’d just lose him on the idea of an ACTUAL motherfreaking paradox. 


Malicious, this smells like something my father’s side of the family would do. The problem is, from where I'm standing - the paradox was created by my mother.  Someone I deeply resent from where I’ve been standing. Criss, he’s had nothing but praise for his family but all I have seen since the start of my universe’s end of the paradox is the X’voor receipts of everyone’s fuck ups. 


“What do you mean?”


“One of your kind is missing their book, the last book of its kind.”


“I don’t understand what that means, I was never told anything about a book.”


“Child, the book is an all encompassing thing that holds tribal spells, memorial information and warnings of the past, present and future. It is never in one singular language and is automatically shifting its text so it can be read. Only those with the blood of Osea have been able to do this, until now.” 


The moment you realize that as a child you were told nothing, and you were forced away from everything your siblings were taught. Because your father wasn’t the same father as your half-brother, and so you were the outcast. Abused, used and thrown away by a man that couldn’t keep his pants on. Part of me was relieved when I died, until now.

 
“Until now? I have the blood but wasn’t taught a lick of it.” 


I probably was told once when I was little, when I was able to actually see my mother when she had custody of me. I barely even got to see my siblings, let alone the times we risked a paradox for a mutual Christmas every other year. The idea of a book, let alone a family memoir that holds some secret - behooves me. Like did mom get into scrapbooking before we all died? 


“You are of royal blood, you don’t need to know to understand. Provided you’re able to come to our aid, the book will hopefully find you.” 


“How, HOW?!? I’m dead - My universe folded in on itself, it took people that weren’t even meant to be on our side! It fused MY version of my sister and Criss’s version together and everything became a mess! It’s all because MOM DECIDED to influence multiple universes to save our kind. She fucked up and made us pay the price, just like her siblings fucked up and made other universes pay the price.”


The voice wasn’t replying, after my rant it had been quite a few minutes before anything else was being said. I realize I keep saying voice, and then vision but it’s not like I have a clue what’s going on. For all I know I could still be dead, and this is the grand joke in the darkness of the end.  


“Your family is not to blame, other forces have been at work against the grain for millenia. We know the history, which is why we come to you.”


MY FAMILY IS NOT TO BLAME? Have you heard of John Kieran ‘Rowe’ X’voor who’s blood boiled so hard his mistakes were blamed on his brothers.  Avery, Xander and T’maz were shoved outside of their main universes. I have more than generational trauma at this stage, I have multiversal paradoxical trauma.  


“You come to me, a transgender dude who’s lost his only kid, lost most of his siblings, was abused by his father and is no longer alive. What am I going to be able to do to fix this? Have you considered grabbing the X’voor line that’s actually alive?”


Another voice started making noises, as far as I’m aware there was only one real voice going on at this stage. I’m confused, why is the afterlife way more confusing? Isn’t this going to be the time that they say I’m at fault because I was sexually assaulted, verbally abused by my father and his side of the family? 


“Kay, you bloody dumbass.” 


“W…who?”


“You don’t know me as who I am right now. I’ve been helping reconnect, or in my words I’ve been chasing you and your brother and some of your siblings. Someone’s got your family book, and some of us are already across this ocean of time and space. I don’t entirely know where this is… But once there’s an easy entrance to get you over here, you won’t be able to find me right away and I likely won’t remember I said this but…” 


This one’s accent was strangely Australian, but not very thick - almost across between an Aussie and an American. I don’t recall anyone this close to me who sounds like this. I mean that in the most sincere way possible, I may get visions in the dark of these tv glitched people telling me my horoscope and how to save my universe but - this guy? 


“Chasing cars and mostly dead ones, sounds like a mental illness, also I strangely don’t know anyone with your accent.”


I was being an ass. I’m confused as shit right now, and as much as I love being the humorous one that points to a house fire and calls your dog crispy… When you shove things in my face, move my cheese and turn my world upside down and shake me? I get wild, rabid and snarky. 


“Kris. You have my protection in another universe, and I regret making your life worse in your universe. Same with your half-brothers.” 


They sound different, and for someone masculine a lot more emotionally attached in the moment than I’d truly expect. The only man I can think of that has this knowledge of me and my brother is Locke. A massive fuckboi, and a truly odd guy, but plagued with mental health issues himself that he couldn’t solve. Probably something to do with the paradox that was slowly swallowing our universe and folding it in like an omelet. 


“Locke?”


“Close.”


“No Cigar?”


“No, I was Locke in your and Criss’s universe. The version of myself you’re hearing right now is a lot older than you, and by the time I make it across the same path I’ll be fused to the me that’s Australian and Celtic. We’re both transgender, much like yourself - but that me was more interested in chasing for emotional reasons. I’m divorced from my cat, but he did give me at least two kids that were reasonable.  Would’ve said three, but Francis is actually my brother’s from the other universe.” 


“Felix?” 


“How’d you know?”


It was a hunch, through the repetition of my death, I experienced visions of other universes and experiences. The events that were shown weren't just my family making serious mistakes to save everything. People, places and things that mirrored just like my universe did to my brother’s.


One of them being a universe where just like here, most of us are dead, and while that seems like paradise at the moment, when you’re stuck inside someone’s head it isn’t some sort of vacation.  Yes, that’s what I said, someone else’s head - another system, a mirrored universe where one of the family mirrors holds the end of the road. I’m sure they’re a lovely person (hopefully) but If whatever is going on doesn’t pan out - kill me fast.


I used to have a subsystem myself, as yes just like Criss, I have dissociative identity disorder. I don’t know how I'd fare being in someone else’s head, let alone someone that’s mirrored or related to me. 


“I saw things between the floods, between the times the tsunami would hit my window and swallow me whole.”


That’s an understatement, it’s been like a multi-universal netflix subscription that I never asked for. I have no way of asking any of my siblings if they’re dead and see the same things, so that universe I just mentioned? Criss and Felix are together, they have kids - but also this Felix? I’m aware of who his ‘cat’ is - mostly by face, I don’t recall the guy’s name. I’m curious how this is going to go. One hand says screw it, stay dead, stay in between the darkness and just float. The other one says a battle’s going to be brewing and we need to go help. 


“That’s interesting.”


“So what’s going on, what’s the end goal?”


“It’s complicated, the person telling you the problem only has a small idea of what’s going on. The book is only PART of the issue, but at the same time it’s the ROOT of the problem. Rather, the book itself is the solution, but the corrupted copies are summoning the souls from every mirror universe it can find. Someone’s trying to cause a paradox, and while the universe you’re about to go into has seen things worse than that… The version that will end up happening if we don’t start trying to find the original book and reverse things, will become a black hole and fuse every universe into one. That’s the best case scenario, the worst case scenario is that nothing will exist, and time and space will eat itself.”  


My heart, or rather what was left of it, was beating - at least to the level of someone who’s dead and no longer alive. I had to be more responsible than I'd ever been, me being the irresponsible baby of one part of the family. After I lost custody of my child, and I faced continual abuse from my father’s side of the family - I gave up on being serious. I had to hide the pain since by then we saw the effects of the paradox. Come to think of it… Are they part of the problem? 


“Are the McNamara's involved?”


I smelled a little more of them after the concept of the book, because there’s no way in hell my family would let something like that go easily. It also explains another theory I had before I died but everyone told me it wasn’t a big deal. That the existing paradoxical issues, the fact that I wasn’t going to see old age because nobody really was going to… Was it not my dad’s family’s problematic meddling? 


“Yes, but also their more dangerous end, the Ardens.” Felix’s voice was quite solemn, as if he were hit by the same issues all of our families were.


‘As if’ - the wonderful understatement of multiple universes. There were times that my father claimed every single one of the children in our family. Criss, while considerably what you’d almost naturally call a prince due to mom’s position in our tribe – spent half his childhood being lied to. Whatever public holidays my father tried to weed his way into, were the ones where Criss’s father was sent to prison for things he didn’t commit. 


“I’m in.” 


My tone shifted from query to determination.  You didn’t have to tell me twice, three times or five times. I need to prevent the damage from getting worse. The amount of damage already caused… I know I keep blaming mom and her husband from the other universe, but I feel like this is because she was trying to re-write the damage my father and his family were doing.


“For what part?”


“All of it, I don’t care how responsible I have to be - I am not letting his family fuck people over more. They’ve controlled the narrative, it’s time we took things back. It’s so bad that in my theory they were even controlling people not from Osenayan blood.” 


Like I said before, the downfall of my people is likely tied to my father, which you’d consider to be a political thing - but in reality it’s far worse than that. It’s the dark occultish magic that lingers like a bad stink bomb in a high school hallway that causes your family to get weird types of cancer at the last minute.  Not that the analogy provided made any sense, but the aesthetic of the situation is basically that they SEEM normal and it seems like a totally normal ‘prank’ until it’s all said and done and a whole lineage is destroyed.


“Ah, you are as your mother described.” 


“...My mom knows you?”


“Kay, a version of your mother may not have been my ex-husband’s mother, but she was in the family. They’re all in connection with each other, those that are higher up in the food chain had a lot of ways to talk to each other. Just like the one that in the world you’re about to move into, they’re aware of more than just your universe.”


“Are you of Osenayan blood like Criss and Kitch and the rest of us?”


“No… well kind of.” 


“You either are or aren’t.” 


That’s the problem, I have a very black and white frame of view because I wasn’t allowed to know much about my heritage. I knew I wasn’t entirely human, but they - just like other members of the family - kept threatening to dock anyone’s non-human parts. With me, that was my cat ears and tail, something that prevented me from both medical care and education on several occasions.


“Right, but me specifically no. The universe where Francis was originally born, yes. It’s complicated. Ask me about it when you find me on the other side.”


I want to know NOW you dumbass, you’re preventing me from seeing the full picture, which universe is it? WHO DO I need to protect and what do I need to know? 


“Why not now.”


“You don’t have time.” 


He was right, the timing was short. I didn’t LIKE that he was right but I was stalling, and there wasn’t any way to get more information before I could make a full choice outside of my anxiety. I knew I needed to do what was asked of me. It was as if the choice was finally being made of me because the darkness was fading and the feeling of the tsunami hitting my walls and windows was going away.


“What’s happening?” I asked as I noticed the dark started to form a path. A multicolored spiritual path of sorts, but it was very visible. At the end of the path was a portal, or rather a big swirly multicolored round thing that could be classified as a doorway or even a halo. It wasn’t exactly solid right now, but I wanted to put my foot down on it to test the amount of pressure I could put down.


“I understand you don’t like your path chosen for you, but you’ll have time to get adjusted, and find your skin within the confines of what’s going on. You won’t find me right away, but when you can search for your brother. You might run into someone that’s like his mirror that’s from that world, they may not be entirely with what’s going on - but once you prove yourself you’ll be able to get more aid.”


“So you mean that I don’t have a choice on things?”


“The place you’re going to save, you’re going to have more responsibility, your ‘job’ won’t just be an office job or a fast food job. You’ll be using magic in a way your mother never expected you’d be able to wield. If you’re interested in ‘jobs’ other than that, you’ll have time to adjust and swap between. You and your brother are considerably in healing positions in the family, and your interest in tarot and astrology has coupled with what you’ll be doing.”


‘Gee KRIS WHY not just learn how to use a band aid!’ my younger sibling once said to me as my knees went weak just trying to wash their wound off after they fell off their bike. Me? A healer? What in god’s green earth do you take me for? Someone with a sense of – oh the caretaking aspect. Yea, I forgot about that.


“I know basic first aid. And by basic I mean I faint when someone has a bloody nose.”


Felix’s voice was gone, and the other person previously was clearly starting to talk before I could even set my feet onto the path in front of me. I don’t understand what’s going on and why it sounds like a D&D campaign. I mean if it’ll be fun to learn something new, but I also want to know why Criss and I are the healing types. I played MMORPG’s before and I rarely ever was able to play a healer. Too much ADHD to pick people off the floor. 


“Child. You’ll be graced with the beginning and end knowledge of the position of Astrologian. You’ll be tasked with practice before you go out in the field, and you will be filled in with everything about this world when you arrive. Once you’ve progressed in your education they will allow you to search for people. Won’t be long, but despite the power and knowledge being there, we need you to be able to DO the things you’re capable of doing.”


Why does this sound like I'm about to go out into Mordor and have a second breakfast and then meet up with Yoda and defeat dragons in space? 


“Wait.. where is the place i’m going?”


“A fantasy journey, one you’d never expect. You’ll become a warrior, and adventurer for your cause. You’ll save not only your kind, but you may just yet reset the dirty deeds of those that have been planning this for millenia.” 


I started to put my foot onto the path in front of me, trying to test the pressure again - more so because I am severely ADHD and you cannot prevent me from bolting before I hear the full story. Except that my foot’s gone through the spirit based path in front of me about five times and the vision, the voice in front of me that’s supposedly my ancestor of sorts - is about done with my inability to pay attention.


“Are you ready?”


Does a bear shit in the woods?


“If it means securing my child's future, and the future children of anyone in every universe ever then yes. I will risk the fact that I think my long list of medications won’t be there for me on the other side… So if I accidentally let someone die because I was staring at a wall, I apologize ahead of time.”
 I tried to walk further onto the path, but besides the voice continuing to instruct me there seemed to be a barrier preventing me from starting my journey. This path finally solidified but by barrier I mean there’s a whole ass invisible wall preventing me from going anywhere.

 
“You’ll be provided with as many equivalent treatments as we are able to ascertain, things may not have the same names as your worlds, but we are far more advanced than most stories may have told you. It is not like the medieval periods of your worlds, we may not have true ‘pharmaceutical’ equivalents, but there are treatments that have been proven.”


So basically insurance doesn’t exist, but you can feed me enough caffeine and energy drinks before my kidney fails me so I can exist. Got it. 
“Ok so let me start the journey, I can’t get further than one foot onto this damn thing.”


“One last thing.”


Am I going to be able to sign my name across someone’s forehead, or heart or something? Sign on the dotted line? What, cause I’m losing my ability to sit still here.


“What, a contract or?”


“Ah, no, would you like to be human and not represent yourself fully for security or would you like to be of similar standing.”


Wait, so you’re putting me through a character creator before I walk across? Can’t I just imagine for you what I need? I don’t want to lose who I’ve become, even if that means parts of who I've been will start leaking out. 


“Cis dude no, but as close to transgender as possible… Cat ears if …. Yea cat like if possible. I don’t have a problem with my brother, but I refuse not to be myself… I’ve fought one too many of my own demons to get this far.” 


“You should run with a tribal member and pick a name when you come in, you will be of the race of Miqo’te. Be it that this is your choice, say the word and you can run as fast as you can to start your quest.”


Dude, I was ready to run when Felix talked about what was going on. I’m ready and I'm running in place, doing jumping jacks like an idiot in case I have to prove I can still breathe. 


“I dunno about names, but names aren’t that big of a deal to me, but let me go - let me find that book and save people. My brother’s usually the leader and the one that organizes things. I want to do right by what he was trying to show me before I died.” 


No response, but the barrier lapsed, and the path became more solid and walkable and not just a spiritual essence. I pressed my foot behind me like the start of a track and field race, like it’s the Osenayan Olympics. I don’t know who I’m going to meet up with, what’s going to happen, but there’s no time like the presence to defeat my father’s family and save the side that wasn’t trying to destroy everyone. I don’t know if we’ll save and fix the past, but if I can save and fix the present and future, it’s better than nothing.


“I guess it’s time.”
