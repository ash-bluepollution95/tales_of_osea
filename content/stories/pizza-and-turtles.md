Rabid, and full of the fuel of anger from the abuse and torment my father’s family pulled on me. Full of the frustration of the violence and gore pulled across multiple universes. I was more than ready, and as soon as I put my foot down one last time… I sprinted. I ran so fast that everything including my own hair, my clothes and the feet beneath me that I wasn’t able to see since I died - were an entire motion based blur.


“I’m coming, Criss, I’m going to save your ass this time. Tell Kitch she has no choice and I’m going to save her ass too. I’m gonna save Locke’s mirror, I’m gonna save everyone. People I don’t know and people I eventually will meet.” 


Turns out that’s going to be one or more extra universes worth of people that will be aware of me and I’ll have almost no time to adjust. I sped through the void, the portal that gave me the entrance to the universe I’m to save…


Before I could even check what I looked like in a mirror, before I could even ascertain what I was wearing… I was bombarded by someone immediately. I wasn’t even able to figure out what or who this was before they pretty much yelled in my face like they knew me. I said before I'd had visions, but I don’t always remember everything from these..


“Uncle Kay. You owe me.” 


A lizard-like girl stood in front of me and poked me in the face. I mean I knew for a fact some of my siblings had kids but none of them were an adult last time I checked. They said this world was going to be different then the one I was from.. 


“Uh….”


Scratch that bit about not remembering, I think it’s starting to fall into place, the pieces of the larger puzzle. The puzzle I was hoping I had more time to solve, because I was more interested in the endgame of saving people than figuring out whose kids were in front of me and what I did wrong.


“YOU WANNA PISS A TURTLE OFF? YOU OWE ME PIZZA.”


Turtle, she grew up watching Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles. She latched onto the character of Michaelangelo because her favorite uncle is Mica. She’s my half-brother’s kid, and she’s the loudest. Well, at least to me right now she’s the loudest.


“I … just got here…”


“Oh shit, you’re not MY Uncle Kay.”


No shit Sherlock, you want to try again kiddo? I mean there’s only a million or more different universes I could come from, and you guessed once already. This isn’t surprising, she’s got my energy wrapped in the most amount of chemicals, sugar and saturday morning cartoons.  


“I … Might be but…”


I tried to act like I might’ve been in case there was a discrepancy.  My full defense for this is that I've been dead for over three years, I'm exhausted. I just got here, you don’t get second and third chances often. 


“No no, that’s not what I meant, you are but you aren’t.  I’m Mimi… er uhh that’s my name here but I’m Maycein.”


I don’t get why there’s a difference in naming. I know she’s Maycein, but why the Japanese name? We’re not in Neo Tokyo or even in Kansas - so why the change of vowels and root words?  


“I saw you in one of my visions, you’re one of the kids that’s of our kind, but are a spiritual essence of sorts?” 


“More like Felix-dad and Criss-dad were dead already and were in a system and had fun and made a bunch of us ‘snotheads’.”


I blinked, and my brain just about fell out of the case of my skull. I don’t even know where I am and I'm being greeted by a lizard. Her accent is quite mixed, but in my visions she was human looking, and had a neutral American accent. Pinks, greens and her weapon is a paintbrush?!?


“That’s a lot of information.”


Lizard, turtle. Lizard, turtle. It’s like I'm counting the sheep before I die again. It’s like I'm counting the lizards before I find the end of the lickable rainbow. The confusing thing is her accent here is not even Asian. She’s got a Japanese name, but her accent sounds European - maybe Russian? 


“Felix is trans like you, and he got his tubes tied after Luke was born HAH. He’s not here thankfully he must’ve been glued to Uncle Finn and Uncle Jamie’s walls.”


“That’s even more information.”


I’m struggling with the mental cork board. I’m meant to be finding where I’m going, who I’m saving and how to handle my future. Yet right now I'm babysitting my niece, being told I owe her pizza. I still don’t even really know what I look like in this world, nor what underwear is trying to crawl up my ass. 


I looked down and around and blinked again, it’s not surprising that my ass-crack is feeling like it’s being grinded by cotton. It’s one of my favorite outfits I wore lounging around the house when I was alive. Baseball style jacket, more like an overshirt, button up - no shirt underneath, and jeans so ratted that you can’t even use them for rags. The underwear problem is being put down to the fact that - I think they gave me a cis body. 


“You’re here to save stuff right? Some of us got here ahead of time, not just because people were summoned, but those of us that were able to copy ourselves over - We asked the ancestor that is in charge to let us help you.”


The room we’re in looks like an apartment, and I’m not sure how this actually equates to a magical entrance.


“Lilac and Luan set this portal up for you, most of us are aware of things - but the book copy people got ahold of Tie and he’s angry and on their side.”


“Where’s … Criss.”


“He’s forgotten who he is, we’re not sure if it’s the books causing this or otherwise. He works in Emperyum, at a bar and at a club. He’s ‘cheating’ on Felix with an Elezen.”


“Where am I going from here… I have to pick gear and stuff up supposedly.” 


Mimi handed me over a folder and an envelope with currency in it and gave me an awkward smile.  She patted me on the head with her paintbrush and gave me one last slip of paper and shoved it in the folder.  It was a joke, hopefully, maybe a threat - maybe an invoice of sorts.


“All your information is in here, but you still owe me pizza. At a later date of course, when you’re able to come back and see anyone of us, there’s details in here of who of us remembers everything and who of us only remembers part - and who’s brain washed like a fucking idiot.” 


“Ah, alright… I should get going, the urgency was put forth before I started running like a jackass into the portal.” 


“Yes, well you’re welcome. Always remember the turtle demands payment for your information, and the payment is pepperoni and cheese.”


“...Mimi you’re not a ninja turtle.”


“EXCUSE ME. NO BUT I AM TURTLE. AND ITS MACE TO YOU. I can’t stand my Auri name, I use it where I have to for respect, but my tribe even here calls me Mace. I get MayMay from the elders, because Mace is a weird name to them.  ANYWAYS I AM LATE FOR A DATE WITH SOMEONE TO ARM WRESTLE FOR ONIGIRI.”


“Do you need money instead?”


“NO. TURTLE MUST WRESTLE.” 


Mimi grinned at me like a whole ass child, and proceeded to zoom at what felt like the speed of light.  After that it was up to me to open the folder and read beyond the invoice of pizza and garlic bread. I’m to find a passage to Ishgard, where I’ll learn more about my job as an Astrologian… Yet it also tells me that I’m becoming a career crafter and that there’s a place in Emperyum not far from Criss that has housing of sorts for me as long as I keep up with the ‘work’. 


“Aren’t I meant to be an adventurer? Not yet? I guess it’s time to go find out what the future’s going to hold then.”


I don’t know where here is, but I walked downstairs from what seemed to be an apartment complex.  I tried to ask people things, but nobody understood what I was asking, and I realized then and there my journey was going to be a lot longer in the beginning than I had even dreamed of.  


Who would’ve thought the journey wasn’t just saving people, but hunting down the unemployed housing and becoming one with the workforce.
