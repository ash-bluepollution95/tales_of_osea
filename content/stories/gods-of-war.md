Life isn’t ever simple, not when you know there’s a paradox trying to swallow the whole of your and several universes like a bad hentai. I’d love to tell you I'm another catboy who only just worked his life in fast food and didn’t do much. I’d love to tell you what you’d love to hear that like my younger brother I dealt with therapy and otherwise. No, instead despite what my family thinks based on what I’ve told them I died before the end of the world. More than once, on different aspects and while that’s confusing, likely even mortifying - I promise you I can explain. 


I don’t know what or who my actual connective mirrors are, never knew and never really cared. I just knew somehow deep inside that my path was to do more. It wasn’t an aesthetic choice of getting educated at a higher level and saving the world with politics and altruism, it was an ancestral heritage choice I had to ease into. I desired more than just to go through track and field in school - running for sport wasn’t enough for me.


The only thing keeping me out of jail was my ancestral ties, and the only thing that kept the government from docking my ears or my tail was these ties. I was some of the last in my universe apart from my younger brother to avoid these incoming laws. I may not have known that much about my heritage down to the wire like other universal family members, but I was taught how to fight.


I knew what they did to my father.


I knew what they did to my mother, and how they tried to do the same with my stepmother.


It wasn’t even legal what was going on, it wasn’t the sort of thing you’d see on local or national television. It was the sort of underground, colloquial dodgy business laws that made people suffer. You didn’t start seeing these laws filter through government politics until me and my younger brother got older. There’s something to be said about the amount of meddling that happened that made things happen faster than they were meant to.


My path to glory, and war wasn’t because of my middle name, I know I can’t stand being called Thomas nor Tommy. It was a later issue when I found out I could do things even alive that I should never have been able to do. According to legends I read in books that my father had stashed away, a child of half blood from Osea shouldn’t be able to fully astrally travel. Not at least while still in the realm of the living.


My father is at least three quarters, if not almost full.


I never got to really meet my grandparents, nor many other siblings of my father’s line because the paradox had felled trees long before I got to even be a teenager. Plucking leaves off the plants decade after decade. People that were healthy one day suddenly came down with rare diseases as if it were biological warfare.


The full reason I didn’t like being called that was because I actually managed to visit the alleyway that backed onto another world of sorts. I called it that because it was usually a way to connect to the other universes.  Once you were able to connect your astral-self to the ‘dock’ of sorts it was like a garage to me.  I was never taught what the true words were, I wasn’t told exactly how to do what I was doing.. I was merely supervised, because I wasn’t going to do anything the safe nor correct way at the rate I was learning to do things.


I knew nothing of WHY this universe smelled different, because each universe sort of had a scent when you knew Osenayans were there. This one had a family that wasn't meant to be there. One that was there due to a court judicial order, but not a natural human court, the court of Osea. At the time I didn’t know that, but by the time I had rolled up, rolled over like a dead dog at the end of the road - I met someone who was meant to be my dad’s cousin of sorts.
The problem with that statement is: My dad was born in a different generation than most of his mirrors. The shuffling confused me, I was looking around each window of the realm to see if one of the ones in his generation were his mirror. I saw my Aunt’s mirror but she was a different generation as well. 


I stood there once in the ‘garage’ area of that realm that stood before the living, and I blinked while I tried to stop understanding why someone was calling for me. Then I realized quickly that somehow I was this man’s partial mirror despite him being another world’s Uncle of the mirror of my father.


“Tommy, don’t argue. We’re here because it’s easier than handling what Kieran did.”

That voice, the one using my name but not speaking to me? Xander, or rather Alexander Maverick Ellis. Technically Xander X’voor, because he’s the current tribal Queen’s brother and he dropped his birth surname after he was removed from the tribal court due to what his older brother did.


“Xan, we’re barely even adults - KIERAN LAWNMOWED HALF A CITY because his anger flipped the Hellaine switch. He was triggered by the protest against the tribal court, and they let HIM stay and because they swear this is a protection order rather than torment - We’re being tormented. We’re here in a state we don’t know, we’re trying to fit in - change our accents and try to re-write things.” 


T’maz Ellis, aka Justin Thomas X’voor. I jokingly call him my arch nemesis, he’s a Generation above me in pecking order. He’s also somehow partly the influence through astral reasons why Thomas is my first name.  Mom says it’s not, but we found out later on that Osea finds a way to influence each child’s name.


I made a loud noise, not realizing people could hear those that were close enough to the living entrance to the world/realm or even universe you’re in.  I tried to run, but when you’re dealing with other people from Osenayan blood heritage, you’re bound to get heard faster than you can say Constantinople. 


“Who’s there?” 


Xander waved a hand and revealed the garage door of sorts. He raised his eyebrow, and saw a young osenayan boy with a toolkit belt and bandana hiding his cat ears. That boy was me at the time, it was the first time I’d met anyone outside of my own family.  The fact that no paradox has plucked people faster in this universe means we have more time right?


At that time, I thought that was the case. 


“Hi… er I ..I can go I’m sorry!” 


I panicked and tried to do the same thing Xander did, but because I don’t know the actual incantations nor the mental framework even today how to control things… I was frozen in fear that I was going to be in trouble. Everything for me was ‘natural’ in a more automated way, I could find the latch, in a somewhat physical way but I couldn’t just wave the door open.


“Take your bandana off.” 


Xander held his hand out towards me and grinned, they were only teenagers at the time themselves.  He was around fourteen or fifteen, and T’maz was closer to sixteen. They’d only been there maybe a few months, it sounded like.  Their accents didn't match the area of the world they lived in.  Let alone the space in the USA they occupied.

  
I took my bandana off, my green and black cloth bandana that had Transformers characters on it. My cat ears popped up really fast, and my hair dropped straight into my face because I forgot to tie it up before putting the bandana on. I made another noise and almost started to cry out of fear, I was likely only eight years old at the time.


“Heh, you’re one of us.” 


T’maz grinned and peeled me, or started to TRY and peel me out of the entry way that Xander uncovered. But due to us being partial mirrors it was like static shock, we wouldn’t kill each other being in the same room, but until more magic is applied - injuries could happen.


“OWWIE.” 


I winced, I was only a child, of course I wasn’t going to handle pain, and now I probably don’t remember how much pain I was in… But I know I was crying for quite a few minutes because I was under the age of ten. I was doing something without supervision because this was one of the first times I knew I could explore. I was mapping out the areas I could access and I knew one day i’d get caught so I could show what I could do.


“Oh…”


T’maz blinked at his hand, and watched Xander help me out of the entrance way and smiled even more. He must’ve put three and four together to realize what was going on, and must’ve had the ability to see what was going on. 


“I’m Jupiter.” 


I blinked a bit and passed my bandana to Xander, even though he didn’t ask for it I was trying to shove it at him because I was always told to give someone something as a gift on my person to remember me by. It was a tribal thing that my Aunt taught me, but I never took it the right way, instead of actually having small relative gifts on my person - I took it as gift things off the small of my back. In other words, someone tells you to take something off and you give it to them happily. 


“Thomas Jupiter Lorcan. Felicity’s middle child.” 


Xander was trying to sound like an adult at that moment. Just like another time later on when I was actually dead that I visited.  Similarly in a different house, but likely the same state in the USA they were in previously - I wanted to warn of the oncoming storm.  I tried to be stealth about it but Xander had my scent on memory. He was already in his thirties and he was just shy of a few weeks before his own death.


“Ah, you’re back.” 


I watched again as Xander opened up the same aesthetic doorway, this time it felt more magical. You’d think I'd pick up a book and learn how to do this the appropriate way, but I prefer doing things the semi mechanical way. 


“Uh hi.” 


“Kiddo, you’re dead, how’d that happen.”


“Leaves, they’re falling.”


Xander sighed and shook his head, he was a father now. He knew just by my words something was coming for him and his own family.  Not just because of the mistakes of his older sister and older brother, but because of the long standing issue with the enemy. 


“Rowan’s stuck his claws in on our side finally hasn’t he. We ain’t got long, this isn’t fair, Taylor’s only three. Maria’s mirror’s kid is only like two.  In fact, Odin just turns two in a few days. Please tell me we have more time. Please tell me my kid’s going to see adulthood and give me time to at least have grandchildren.”


“Who’s Rowan?” 


I didn’t know the family line very well. I spent most of my time still mapping everything in case I couldn’t find the solution. I didn’t know anything about the reasons why paradoxes are plucking people off left and right. I knew it was happening, but I didn’t understand the magic behind it.


“It’s hard to explain because your universe isn’t a 1-1 match, but Maria, my sister, married someone that wasn’t of Osenayan blood. This is fine normally, but it ended up being the Arden-Mcnamara line. The one that’s been involved with creating the paradoxes that damage more than the tiny little mistakes our families make.”


“Are they the ones that made my dad lose his marbles?”


“Yea, that’d be about it.”


I started to growl, the sheer fact that someone was responsible for the downfall patterns of my parents. It wasn’t easy to take in, but it solidified the fact that there was a war coming and I was going to fight. I did my best to train when I was alive, but how much of that i’ve retained as a floaty toasty ghost by now I don't know. 


“Do you know why you’re named the way you are?”


“Why are you asking me this now, I'm dead and I'm what, twenty nine now??”


“Hah, because you don’t ask the living in Osenayan tradition even in English why you’re named the way you are. We don’t learn these things until the living has passed, and it’s important because you’ll learn that about other family members. Were you given an Osenayan name?”


I thought for a moment, you could envision a digital spinner on top of my forehead, the typical math symbols flying in space. By now, Frank or Francis as mom would still call him - would kick me in the shin and tell me to stop wasting my brain cells. I’d ask him why we let my younger brother get away with that and he’d tell me it was because he had none. 


“Uh, no no I was not.”


“My brother is T’maz.  You’re likely T’mai J’ptah. Do you know your surname besides your safe one?’


“SAFE…. Surname?”


Turns out there’s a combination of societal rules, you have legal english surnames so you’re able to get around things legally.  I wouldn’t know, I never used my dad’s last name because my parents weren’t married until Aspen was born.


“I take that as no. Universally we’re not all from the same tribe, but we are some form of family.  Sadly I don’t have the same power as my sister, so I can’t just look into your past to see which tribe you are and what your connection is.”


“Tribe of the Sealy Posturepedic and Taco Bell.” 


I grinned like a dumbass, I didn’t know shit for squat about literals about my family, that stuff was hidden from my father. We had a little bit of information in the form of family photos or genealogy records, but it was harder to get than even someone who was Native American heritage. All I knew is my dad was like the dirtiest heinz 57 varieties of fucked up according to his wording. 


“God you sound like my nephew from the universe I'm meant to be from, they’re safe for now - I have the ability to connect with my sister. But she’s aware of Rowan’s plans, and while she’s struggling to stay married for other reasons beyond legal troubles with Mikhail aka Mahal’ya.”


I just kept grinning.


“T’mai J’ptah Lorcan. You have a wild path ahead of you, is this the first time you’ve been ghosted?” 


“Nope, just the first time I’ve been permanently ghosted. WELL BEFORE I INTENDED TO scarily, but this was not because of an issue with that other enemy family..”


“No, I smell a lack of brain cells on your part, but that’s not surprising. You’re a child of war, and you’re here, why?” 


“I … got hit by a truck because I was drinking with my friends instead of out training like I promised my older brother I was doing.” 
“No, why are you here besides that.”


What are you asking me? I told you WHY I ended up here this time sort of - I don’t know why I'm here otherwise. It’s not something you can scrape out of me when I barely understand how to progress out of the mapping loop.


“I don't know how to stop mapping the paths to every universe, my younger brother’s gonna die soon and he' s meant to take over. Aspen’s been shown roughly how to do this but he’s struggled with his mental health and barely remembers anything.”


“Jupiter, time is fluid at the moment, when you’re needed as a soldier you’ll be scooped up and put in the right position. You have the soul marking of the soldiers in our heritage, it sounds like your father’s heritage tribes are akin to the war and protection classes I was taught about as a child.”


Xander’s face was so solemn, so calm at this stage. I don’t understand why he’s not worried about me or Aspen not being able to do anything yet. They could die before we can save them. It didn’t dawn on me until much later like now, while I’m finally sitting at a table falling asleep where my boss is wondering why I'm snoring.


I hallucinate in my sleep, which likely means I'm travelling in different parts of my life across the astral realm. I should’ve said this sooner, I know I'm not making any sense of this but I barely understand where I am when I'm awake.


“J’upye Tia, why are you asleep on the job again?” 


A man with cat ears, pink and blue braided long hair stood before me in his battle gear that doubled as his work uniform. He’s not my boss, but he acts like he is. I know more about him than he’d like to admit. 


“What do you think?”


“If I catch you astral travelling on the job again I'll have to report you to Angel.” 


“Wait, how’d you know?”


“I’m having one of my more lucid days you could say.”


That’s weird, every other time I’ve been caught he just realizes I’m tired and walks off and doesn’t poke at me. He rarely remembers who I was when I was alive, as he doesn’t really know me. But when he’s more lucid, I realize he’s the Queen’s son. At least of the over-arching tribal authority.  Ma’ra’ii’s son Matoya’iivi on occasion uses his heritage abilities to sniff out another like himself. 


“If you’d let me do what I’m here to do I’d be able to save us faster.”


I raised my eyebrow at him, and grumbled. 


“You’re a purple haired, pink eyed, punk of a millennial like myself. You’re not allowed to rush. You’re meant to keep watch when I'm not lucid, that’s why Angel has you employed. He’s not from Osea, but he’s connected for reasons.  These people are from this world, some are from Osea, some are just as precious. You learn the more you’re here no matter how much a world is imagined up, it exists in time and space.”


“Yea but you’re my dad’s mirror.”


“Your dad’s a war mongering assfuck somedays. Don’t think I haven’t spent some of my worst days before therapy cleaning my mind – before coming here – hallucinating my way through the lines just like you. Your mother is likely chasing my tail here, because of a mirror of theirs from my world. Best be on the lookout, and keep your nose clean.”


I swear to you on a stack of bibles Criss was never this sarcastic and pissy before, is this what it’s like meeting someone with Dissociative Identity Disorder? I don’t understand why he’s in a lucid mode, and trying to confuse me more.


“Right, but I know the task, you don’t. My task isn’t just you, my task is more than that.”


“Your task is waiting it out until I have the strength to defeat the curse on me, and then we’ll be on the job together. My half brother may be a healer, but he’s too ADHD to stick to something. I’m aware when I'm lucid where everything has stood since coming here, if you promise me you’ll keep a secret – I'll tell you where I keep this information.” 


“Do you actually trust me more than your cook?”


“More than I can trust him to clean his sleeping bag which is now sitting outside hanging from the viewing deck. Angel told him to get it cleaned last week, and it’s just even more stink filled than it was the week before.” 


“I gather your cook is your other worldly child.”


“I can’t confirm that yet, but that’s because even when I'm lucid I don’t have all the information. He doesn’t always work when I'm on shift, and most of the time off shift he’s on the computer systems gaming.” 


I nodded finally and stood up, knowing I had a job to do long term and I had to settle down and wait things out. Hard to do when you have a path in front of you to learn and serve, but there’s shit to do. I gotta listen to what I'm told, and if it doesn’t mesh, then I’ll buck it and do something else. This is the thing, I know he’s not bullshitting me, he can be a leader type when he’s able to.  From what I heard, this curse on him was when he was alive, as he and his sister were meant to be the next in line. 


“Ok well, i’ll do my best, i’m assuming you need me to do something with your information you’ve been gathering?” 


“Yes, keep it safe, categorize it, if you know anything from your travels, document it alongside. This is important, you’re not meant to do as much travel as you did before - you mapped it all out, spare yourself the energy. I assume you have an understudy to help you with your mapping that you haven’t finished.” 
     
 
War is about planning the final moves, and I realize that while I'm not a General, nor a corporal, I'm just a Private in a large army at this stage… I’m serving my purpose, and I’m happy to help save people if it requires me doing menial work to set up positions for a better percentage chance to win.
