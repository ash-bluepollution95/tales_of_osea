# Tales of Osea

A website for the FFXIV Machinima Project by Under Rug Swept Misfits.

## About The Project

Tales of Osea: A series mirroring the concept of multiversal selves, fictionalizing the world and feelings of those that may have once lived before. 

Much like the sundered worlds, the cracks of other worlds start to show - and the differences start flooding in.

## About Us

We are the Duskfall Portal Crew, a DID system with over 300 alters, navigating life with DID, ADHD, Autism, and CPTSD. We believe in gaming, and many other medium's potential to break down barriers and enhance mental health, despite challenges. Join us on our creative journey exploring identity and expression.
